Video editor & architect.

I’m Yousef Abokamar, a video editor and architectural engineer from Egypt. For more than two years I’ve been editing social reels, promotional videos and brand content, working with Sorah Media Solutions and with clients directly as a freelancer.

My work has reached brands in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Bahrain and Oman, across real estate, medical, sports, tech and events. My architecture background shapes how I edit: clear composition, clean rhythm and a story that fits each brand.
